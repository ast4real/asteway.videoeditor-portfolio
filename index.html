About me

I'm Asteway Melese, a video editor who turns raw footage into stories people watch to the end. I care about rhythm: cutting on the beat, letting a shot breathe when it should, and making sound and picture work as one.

My work ranges from music-driven edits to cinematic everyday-life pieces with a nostalgic, filmic look. Each project starts with the feeling the video should leave, then I build the pacing, color and sound to match.

I'm open to freelance work and collaborations. Message me on Telegram or email to talk about your project.
